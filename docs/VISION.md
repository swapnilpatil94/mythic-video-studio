# Product Vision

## Product

Mythic Video Studio is a local-first storytelling and animation pipeline for Hindi mythology content.

The final videos should feel like **premium hand-illustrated storytelling**, not stock footage and not crude stick-figure whiteboard animation.

Visual DNA:
- cream/parchment base
- expressive black ink linework
- restrained gold/red accents
- detailed Indian-inspired illustration
- hand-drawn reveals
- dynamic camera movement
- strong typography
- visual variety every few seconds
- respectful treatment of sacred/revered figures

## Content ladder

### Stage A — Short validation
60–90 seconds, 10–25 visual beats.

### Stage B — Stress test
3 Shorts in one run.

### Stage C — Long-form validation
8–12 minutes, roughly 1,500–1,900 Hindi/translated-word-equivalent narration depending on delivery pace, with 120–180 visual beats.

### Stage D — Serialized production
A source-grounded season, potentially using one selected text/source at a time (e.g. a Shiv Purana section), with continuity across episodes.

## Creative roles

Frontier/Work:
- research director
- story architect
- script writer
- continuity director
- visual director
- quality reviewer

Local pipeline:
- asset generator
- image processor
- TTS runner
- animation compositor
- renderer
- validator

Human:
- final editorial approval
- source/cultural review where appropriate
- publish decision

## Non-goals

- no documentary/B-roll-first format
- no 200 unique AI images for 200 shots
- no fixed global character library that prevents new characters
- no one-shot mega-prompt for an entire 10-minute production
